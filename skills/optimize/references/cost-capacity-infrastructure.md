# Cost, Capacity, And Infrastructure Optimization

Inspect utilization, quotas, retention, idle resources, scaling, saturation,
headroom, request limits, throttling, queue depth, placement, egress, storage,
compute shape, startup cost, reservations, and high-availability overhead.
Compare provisioned, configured, requested, and observed capacity.

Do not equate low average utilization with waste when bursts, failover,
maintenance, recovery objectives, or commitments justify headroom. Do not
recommend SKU, region, topology, or redundancy changes without current
availability, quota, price, compatibility, reliability, and migration evidence.
