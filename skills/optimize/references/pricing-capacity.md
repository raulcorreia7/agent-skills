# Prices, Limits, And Capacity

Pricing, quota, capacity, service limits, availability, and product behavior are
current external facts. Verify them for the exact currency, agreement context,
region, SKU, tier, effective date, and usage model. Public retail prices are not
the organization's negotiated or realized cost. A documented maximum is not
the current quota or available regional capacity.

Model uncertainty with ranges and sensitivity analysis. Show which assumption
changes the recommendation and omit false precision.
