# Practice maturity

Classify a practice or tool by the evidence available:

1. Lead. Use a search result, summary, vendor page, forum post, or anecdote only
   to find attributable evidence.
2. Candidate. Confirm that the evidence is attributable, relevant, current
   enough, and direct. Label provisional sources.
3. Corroborated. Require agreement from a genuinely independent source or
   method. Treat copied, syndicated, or mutually dependent reports as one
   evidence chain.
4. Supported in context. Confirm the target version, environment, constraints,
   and decision. Check comparable operational use, failures, and counterevidence.
5. Battle-tested or default. Require sustained use across independent
   organizations or implementations, maintained artifacts, visible failure
   history, and acceptable security and compatibility outcomes.

Record consensus separately. Consensus can support terminology or a normative
choice, but it does not by itself prove effectiveness or operational maturity.
Reassess a classification when the evidence, version, context, or maintenance
state changes.
