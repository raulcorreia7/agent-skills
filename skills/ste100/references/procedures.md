# Procedures

These rules apply to procedures, including the procedure structure of safety
text.

## Procedure Rules — 5.1–5.5

| Rule | Operational check | Official PDF pages |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Keep each procedure or safety sentence to 20 counted words. A note sentence can have 25 words. | 87–88 |
| 5.2 | Give non-simultaneous actions separate numbered work steps. One sentence normally has one instruction. Combine only simultaneous actions. | 87–89 |
| 5.3 | Use an imperative command. Do not add `must` to an ordinary imperative. Reserve it for safety-important instructions or important conditions. | 87, 89–90 |
| 5.4 | Put a necessary or special prerequisite condition first and separate it from the command with a comma. | 87, 90–91 |
| 5.5 | Keep notes informational. Put instructions, requirements, limits, tolerances, safety content, and necessary results in the applicable work step. | 87, 91–93 |

A work step can contain more than one sentence only for a simultaneous action
or its immediate result or limit. Give each step a visible number or letter.
Put a limit, tolerance, or necessary result directly after its action.

Remove every note and read the procedure again. Move information necessary for
safe execution into a work step. Repeat the test. A note must not use an
imperative.

Example:

```text
1. Open the access panel.
2. Remove the filter.
```

Source: Rules 5.1–5.5, official PDF pages 87–93.
