# Official-Directive Precedence

STE controls expression. Use it together with applicable publication
specifications, style guides, contracts, and other official directives:

- Rule 1.14 defaults to American English spelling, but an official directive
  can require another spelling. Do not change spelling in immutable text.
- STE does not define an abbreviation system. Use approved company or project
  abbreviations and introduce them as the applicable directive requires.
- STE does not regulate typeface, numbering, lettering, or other formatting.
- STE does not select a notation system for units of measurement.
- Safety labels and visual formatting come from the applicable risk analysis
  and publication directive. Rules 7.1–7.3 control their language content.
- For punctuation not specified by Section 8, use the applicable style guide.

Example: If the project specification requires British spelling, keep
`colour`. Do not change it to the STE default spelling `color`.

Source: The general introduction is on official PDF pages 36–37. Rule 1.14 is
