# Clear-Writing Sources

- [ASD-STE100 Issue 9](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf)
  is a controlled natural language standard for technical documentation, not a
  general-purpose style guide.
- George Orwell's [“Politics and the English Language”](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/)
  gives flexible advice for general prose. The Orwell Foundation publishes this
  authorized text with permission from the Orwell Estate.
